#### 3. Searchable Interactive FAQ Accordion
Include a search bar filtering through these 7 accordion items with complete, professional answers:
1. **How do I connect my WhatsApp number?**
   - Answer: Go to Settings > WhatsApp Integration > Scan the QR Code using your WhatsApp Business application.
2. **How can patients book appointments?**
   - Answer: Patients can book via your custom clinic web link, WhatsApp AI assistant chat, or direct walk-in token at reception.
3. **How do I see all booked appointments?**
   - Answer: Navigate to the "Appointments" tab in the left sidebar to view calendar, daily queue, and booking history.
4. **How can I edit Organization details?**
   - Answer: Go to Settings > Clinic Profile to update your clinic name, address, doctor timings, and consultation fees.
5. **What should I do if I'm not receiving patient messages?**
   - Answer: Check your WhatsApp connection status in Settings. If disconnected, re-scan the QR code or verify active AI credits.
6. **How can I contact support?**
   - Answer: Use the WhatsApp chat link above, send an email to support, or fill out the Support Form below.
7. **How do I train the AI assistant for better responses?**
   - Answer: Go to Settings > AI Receptionist > Knowledge Base to upload custom FAQs, clinic policies, and doctor specialties.

#### 4. Support Hours Banner
- Display a clean info card:
  - **Support Hours:** Monday - Friday: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (IST / PKT)

#### 5. Support Request Modal & Form
- Section Title: "Need Help or Want to Share Feedback?"
- Instructions Card:
  - Step 1: Click "Open Support Form"
  - Step 2: Select the type of request
  - Step 3: Fill in the required details
  - Step 4: Submit the form
- CTA Button: `Open Support Form` (Triggers a Shadcn Dialog Modal).
- **Modal Fields:**
  - Request Type Dropdown: `Bug Report`, `Feature Improvement Request`, `General Help & Support`
  - Subject Input field
  - Detailed Description Textarea
  - Priority Level (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`)
  - Submit Button with loading state connecting to Next.js Server Action (`submitSupportTicket`).
